Speed postings apply to the roadway and direction for which they are displayed. A ramp, frontage road, or connecting street may carry a different posting even when it runs beside a higher-speed route.

Read changes early and adjust without abrupt braking. A maximum posting remains subject to conditions, while a lower temporary or location-specific posting may control within its marked area. State callouts provide the values used by local law.

## Reading a change

- Confirm the sign faces your direction of travel.
- Note whether a plaque limits the posting to a condition.
- Reach the new speed through a smooth controlled adjustment.
- Keep scanning for another posting after a transition.
