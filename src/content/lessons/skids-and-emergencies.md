Vehicle emergencies become harder when panic produces abrupt steering or braking. Look toward open space, keep a firm controlled grip, and make the smallest input that restores a stable path.

When traction is lost, reduce the demand on the tires and avoid fighting the slide with a sudden correction. For a mechanical problem, maintain directional control first, signal or warn others when possible, and move out of traffic before troubleshooting.

## Priority order

- Keep the vehicle pointed toward a safe path.
- Reduce speed without destabilizing the vehicle.
- Communicate with surrounding traffic.
- Stop where people and the vehicle are protected.
