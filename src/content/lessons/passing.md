Passing compresses several decisions into a short period: roadway permission, sight distance, oncoming speed, the lead vehicle's movement, and a safe return gap. If any part cannot be confirmed, wait.

Do not begin where markings, signs, curves, hills, crossings, or traffic make the path uncertain. Signal before leaving and returning. Reenter only after establishing clear space from the passed vehicle rather than cutting into its stopping margin.

## Abort early

- Recheck the opposing path immediately before moving out.
- Expect the lead vehicle to change speed or direction.
- Abandon the pass if the available space begins to shrink.
- Never pressure another driver into speeding.
