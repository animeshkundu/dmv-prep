Parking maneuvers are slow but visually demanding. Begin from a position that provides room to steer, signal the intent, and check for people or vehicles before every change in direction.

Use very low speed so steering corrections remain small and stopping is immediate. Mirrors help with alignment, but direct checks are still needed for low objects and road users outside the mirror view.

## Finish safely

- Center the vehicle within the usable space.
- Straighten the wheels unless terrain or local rules call for another position.
- Set the parking brake before releasing control.
- Check traffic and the blind area before opening a door or leaving the space.
