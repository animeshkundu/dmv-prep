An entrance lane provides space to observe traffic, build an appropriate speed, and select a gap. Search early and coordinate with through traffic rather than reaching the end of the lane without a plan.

For an exit, move into the correct lane before the departure point and use the deceleration area for most slowing when conditions allow. If an exit is missed, continue to the next safe route instead of stopping or crossing a separation area.

## Merge plan

- Identify a gap while speed is still adjustable.
- Check the blind area before crossing the lane line.
- Avoid forcing through traffic to brake sharply.
- Preserve extra room around large vehicles.
