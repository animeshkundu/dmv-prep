Warning signs describe a condition ahead rather than granting priority or ordering a specific maneuver. The best response is to identify what will change, decide where the conflict may appear, and create time to react.

An advisory speed or distance plaque adds context to the warning. Treat it as information for planning, not as permission to ignore conditions that call for an even more cautious approach.

## Hazard translation

- Name the condition shown by the symbol.
- Search beyond the sign for the actual hazard.
- Adjust before entering the affected area.
- Leave room for a road user who may be hidden by the condition.
