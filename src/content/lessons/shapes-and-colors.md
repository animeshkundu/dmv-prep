A sign's outline is designed to remain useful when distance, glare, or weather makes its message hard to read. Distinctive silhouettes can signal a required stop, a required yield, a warning, or a special crossing before the legend becomes clear.

Color narrows the likely purpose but should be combined with shape, symbols, and placement. Train yourself to notice the whole sign rather than memorizing a single visual feature.

## Practice cues

- Scan the outline before reading.
- Use the background color to identify the broad family.
- Confirm the meaning from the symbol or legend.
- Keep watching the roadway while processing the sign.
