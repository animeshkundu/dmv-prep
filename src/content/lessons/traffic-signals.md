A signal indication controls movement, but it does not clear the path. Before proceeding, check that cross traffic has stopped and that no pedestrian, cyclist, or vehicle remains in the intended path.

A permissive indication allows movement only when the route through the intersection is available. Do not enter if congestion would leave the vehicle blocking cross traffic or a crossing area.

## Signal routine

- Identify which signal head applies to your lane.
- Read arrows separately from circular indications.
- Scan the conflict area before moving.
- Finish a lawful movement predictably if the indication changes after entry.
