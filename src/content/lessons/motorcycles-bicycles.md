Motorcycles and bicycles are easy to miss because they occupy less visual space, not because they are necessarily far away or moving slowly. Look specifically for them at intersections, before opening a door, and before changing lanes.

They need room to avoid surface hazards and may shift position within a lane. Treat the whole lane as occupied. Pass only when the path is lawful and enough lateral and forward clearance can be maintained.

## Search habits

- Recheck after the first glance.
- Avoid judging speed from apparent size alone.
- Expect movement around drains, debris, and rough pavement.
- Leave a following gap that supports smooth braking.
