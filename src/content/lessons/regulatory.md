Regulatory signs communicate rules that govern the movement or use of traffic. Their message may require an action, prohibit a movement, reserve a lane, or establish how traffic proceeds at a controlled location.

Read the complete message early enough to comply smoothly. A symbol with a prohibition mark changes the meaning of the underlying symbol, and an arrow or supplemental plaque may limit the rule to a direction, lane, or condition.

## Effective response

- Identify whether the sign requires or prohibits an action.
- Check arrows and plaques for scope.
- Make the needed lane or speed adjustment gradually.
- Do not wait until the sign is beside the vehicle to react.
