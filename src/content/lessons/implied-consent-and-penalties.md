Implied-consent systems connect the driving privilege to chemical testing under conditions set by local law. Refusal can trigger a licensing consequence that is separate from any criminal case, and it does not necessarily prevent other evidence from being used.

Penalty structures vary by jurisdiction, prior history, measured concentration, injury, and other circumstances. Use state callouts for the governing rule and avoid merging suspension, revocation, fines, treatment, and criminal sanctions into a single consequence.

## Read carefully

- Identify whether a question concerns refusal or a conviction.
- Separate administrative licensing action from court punishment.
- Check whether the rule changes with prior events.
- Use the cited local provision for exact consequences.
