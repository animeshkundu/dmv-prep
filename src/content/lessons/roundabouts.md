Roundabout decisions begin before the yield line. Read destination signs and lane arrows early, choose the lane that serves the intended exit, and reduce speed while searching the circulating roadway and nearby crossings.

Yield to traffic already circulating and enter only when a safe gap is available. Once inside, maintain the chosen lane unless markings direct otherwise. Signal the exit and watch for people crossing beyond the circular roadway.

## Stable path

- Do not stop inside unless traffic conditions require it.
- Never pass a large vehicle that needs additional tracking space.
- If the intended exit is missed, continue around rather than making a sudden correction.
