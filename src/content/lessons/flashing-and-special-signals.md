Flashing indications require a different response from steady indications. Read the color and symbol together, then apply the stopping or yielding behavior associated with that display.

Special signals may control a particular lane, movement, crossing, or transit operation. Their position matters: confirm that the signal governs your path rather than a neighboring lane.

## Avoid common errors

- Do not treat every flashing display as the same instruction.
- Search for people using an activated crossing.
- Check overhead lane controls before entering a reversible or managed lane.
- Follow an authorized traffic controller when temporary direction replaces the signal.
