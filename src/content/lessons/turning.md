A safe turn starts with early lane selection and a clear signal. Reduce speed while the vehicle is still traveling straight, then search the sidewalk, crossing, bicycle space, opposing flow, and receiving lane.

Use a controlled path that does not cut across an unintended lane or swing wide into another road user's space. State rules can vary for turns at a red indication, so use the local fact callout rather than assuming a familiar rule applies everywhere.

## Turn sequence

- Position before slowing sharply.
- Search the crossing immediately before moving.
- Keep a speed that allows steering control.
- Finish in the lane assigned by markings and local rules.
