Alcohol and other impairing substances can reduce judgment, tracking, reaction, vision, and coordination before a person feels obviously impaired. Combining substances can make the effects stronger or less predictable.

A legal concentration threshold is an enforcement rule, not a promise of safe driving below it. State callouts provide the applicable values. The reliable plan is to separate substance use from driving and arrange transportation before impairment begins.

## Planning choices

- Select a sober driver in advance.
- Keep keys away from an impaired person.
- Do not estimate readiness from confidence or appearance.
- Include prescription and over-the-counter warnings in the decision.
