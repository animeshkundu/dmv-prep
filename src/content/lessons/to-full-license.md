Graduated licensing separates learning, independent driving with restrictions, and full privileges. Passing a test may advance the driver to a new phase without ending every condition.

Track the phase name, effective date, passenger limits, time restrictions, violation consequences, and any remaining education or practice requirement. These details vary by jurisdiction and can change with age or driving history, so use the state callouts and current licensing source.

## Advancement check

- Complete every holding and practice requirement.
- Verify which restrictions continue after the road test.
- Keep proof of completed education or practice.
- Recheck the rule after a citation or interruption.
