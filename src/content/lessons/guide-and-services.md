Guide signs organize route choices, destinations, exits, and services. Their value comes from early reading: first find the destination or route, then match it to the arrow or lane indication.

If a turn or exit is missed, continue to a safe legal route correction. Sudden braking, reversing, or crossing a painted separation creates far more risk than taking the next available connection.

## Reading order

- Locate the destination or route marker.
- Match the message to its arrow.
- Move into the proper lane when a safe gap exists.
- Keep a steady path if the maneuver can no longer be made safely.
