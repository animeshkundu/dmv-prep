After a collision, stop and assess immediate danger before focusing on vehicle damage. Call for help when anyone may be injured, warn approaching traffic when that can be done safely, and avoid standing in an exposed travel path.

Exchange the information required locally and record objective details such as location, vehicle positions, visible damage, and witness contacts. Do not argue about fault at the scene. Reporting thresholds vary, so use the state callout and cited rule.

## Scene priorities

- Protect injured people from additional harm.
- Follow lawful instructions from responders.
- Move vehicles only when safe and permitted.
- Preserve accurate notes without obstructing traffic.
