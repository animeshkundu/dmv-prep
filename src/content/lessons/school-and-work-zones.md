Special zones combine lower margins with more complex movement. Children, workers, equipment, turning vehicles, and traffic queues may enter the path from places that are hard to see.

Use signs, beacons, plaques, and authorized direction to determine when the zone controls. The state callout supplies any variable speed rule. Regardless of the posted value, create enough time and space to respond to a person or stopped queue.

## Zone scan

- Look beyond the first sign for the actual activity.
- Expect a queue over a hill or around a curve.
- Follow a flagger or other authorized controller.
- Avoid lane changes that crowd workers or crossing users.
