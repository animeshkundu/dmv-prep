Crossing users can be hidden by parked vehicles, stopped traffic, roadway geometry, or another turning vehicle. Slow the search down before the conflict point and check both the near and far sides of the road.

Keep the crossing clear when traffic ahead is stopped. When yielding, allow the person to complete the relevant path without pressure from creeping or close passing. Be especially cautious when another vehicle has stopped near a crossing; it may be screening someone from view.

## Search pattern

- Scan sidewalks and curb areas before turning.
- Recheck the path after looking for motor traffic.
- Expect different walking speeds and unpredictable movement.
- Do not wave someone into a path you cannot confirm is safe.
