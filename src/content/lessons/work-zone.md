Temporary traffic controls may shift lanes, reduce available roadway space, stop traffic, or place workers close to moving vehicles. Follow the temporary signs and devices even when they differ from the familiar permanent alignment.

Look well beyond the first device for queues, equipment, flaggers, and merging traffic. Smooth speed changes and generous following space reduce the chance that a small delay becomes a collision.

## Work-zone habits

- Treat temporary markings and channelizers as the current path.
- Expect the layout to change from a previous trip.
- Avoid distractions while workers and equipment are nearby.
- Preserve a safe escape path around stopped traffic.
