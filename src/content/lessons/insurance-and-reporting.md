Financial-responsibility rules identify the coverage or other proof a driver or vehicle must maintain. Minimum liability values differ by jurisdiction and policy period, so use the state callout rather than memorizing a national number.

A police report, agency crash report, and insurance claim serve different purposes and may have different deadlines. Keep records, notify the appropriate organization promptly, and provide accurate facts. Do not assume that contacting an insurer completes a government reporting duty.

## Recordkeeping

- Keep current proof available as local law requires.
- Record policy and contact information after a collision.
- Check the cited state rule for reporting triggers.
- Preserve documents related to repairs and medical care.
