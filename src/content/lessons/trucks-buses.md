Large vehicles have broad areas that the driver cannot see directly and need more space to stop, turn, and recover from traffic changes. If the driver's mirror cannot be seen, assume your vehicle may not be visible.

Do not move into the inside of a wide turn or cut closely in front after passing. On grades and in wind, expect speed and lane position to change. Create space instead of lingering beside the vehicle.

## Visibility plan

- Pass efficiently without exceeding a safe speed.
- Allow the full trailer path to clear before returning.
- Stay out of the space a bus needs to serve a stop.
- Avoid following so closely that the roadway ahead disappears.
