People walking or using small mobility devices may be hidden by vehicles, street furniture, or landscaping. Their direction can change quickly, and they may need extra time or space to cross.

Animals can react to noise and movement in ways a driver cannot predict. Slow well before the encounter, avoid sounding the horn nearby unless needed to prevent immediate danger, and follow the direction of a rider or handler when present.

## Conflict reduction

- Search the roadway edge as well as the lane ahead.
- Expect a person to continue across an intersecting driveway.
- Give a scooter or mobility user room for surface hazards.
- Avoid squeezing through a gap that depends on perfect behavior.
