When lights or a siren are detected, first determine the responder's direction and likely path. Signal, move predictably toward a safe stopping position, and avoid blocking an intersection or access point.

At a stopped roadside scene, search for workers, equipment, open doors, and people stepping into a lane. Use the applicable lane-change or slowing rule shown in the state callout, and never make an abrupt maneuver that transfers risk to nearby traffic.

## Key habits

- Reduce audio distractions so warning devices can be heard.
- Check mirrors before changing position.
- Leave space for more than one responding vehicle.
- Resume only after the path and surrounding traffic are clear.
