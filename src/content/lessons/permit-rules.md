A learner permit is a supervised phase with conditions that can depend on age, supervisor qualifications, passengers, time, location, and the purpose of travel. Read all conditions together rather than treating the permit as a limited version of a full license.

Eligibility ages, holding periods, and practice requirements vary. State callouts supply those values. Keep an accurate practice record and make sure the supervising driver and vehicle meet local requirements before each trip.

## Permit check

- Confirm the permit is valid for the planned trip.
- Identify who may supervise.
- Check passenger and time restrictions.
- Carry any records or proof required for the phase.
