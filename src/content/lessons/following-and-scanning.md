Scanning is an active cycle: look far ahead for developing problems, check the space immediately around the vehicle, and return attention to the primary path. Fixating on the lead vehicle hides the events that will make it brake.

Following space should expand when visibility, traction, traffic complexity, or driver condition reduces response margin. Keep enough room to slow gradually and retain a side escape path when possible.

## Scan cycle

- Look through and around the vehicle ahead.
- Check mirrors regularly without staring.
- Notice brake lights several vehicles forward.
- Rebuild space when another vehicle enters the gap.
