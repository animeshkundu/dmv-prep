Parking restrictions protect sight lines, emergency access, crossings, loading activity, and the usable width of the road. A space that physically fits a vehicle is not necessarily a lawful or safe parking place.

Read signs, curb markings, and nearby controls before leaving the vehicle. Distances from hydrants, crossings, signs, and rail facilities vary by jurisdiction, so use the state fact callouts rather than transferring a number from memory.

## Clear-space test

- Keep crossing and ramp access open.
- Avoid screening a sign or another road user.
- Leave emergency and service access usable.
- Check whether a restriction applies only during a stated condition.
