A left turn crosses several possible paths: opposing traffic, people in the crossing, cyclists near the roadway edge, and vehicles entering the receiving road. Search each path before accepting a gap.

Signal early and position the vehicle predictably. Wait with the wheels aligned for the intended approach, then turn only when the whole path is clear. Finish in the proper receiving lane without cutting the corner.

## Gap check

- Compare the speed and distance of opposing traffic.
- Search the crossing again just before moving.
- Keep room to complete the turn without stopping in a conflict area.
- Yield when another road user would need to brake or change course for you.
