Rain, snow, ice, fog, darkness, and glare reduce what the tires or the driver can do. Slow before traction or visibility becomes limited, increase following space, and use gentle steering, braking, and acceleration.

Drive so the vehicle can stop within the distance that is clearly visible. Keep glass and lamps clean, use the appropriate beam, and look toward the roadway edge rather than directly into oncoming glare. State callouts provide variable headlight requirements.

## Margin check

- Test grip with small smooth inputs.
- Avoid cruise control when traction is uncertain.
- Increase space before a curve or hidden queue.
- Stop in a safe place if visibility no longer supports travel.
