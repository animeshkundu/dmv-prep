A posted limit does not promise that the roadway can be traveled safely at that speed under every condition. Visibility, traction, traffic density, curves, surface damage, and the presence of vulnerable road users can all require a lower speed.

Choose speed by asking whether the vehicle can stop within the clear path ahead and remain stable through the next hazard. Adjust before entering the problem area; braking after grip or visibility is lost may be too late.

## Conditions check

- Look for reduced sight distance.
- Notice changes in surface and weather.
- Leave more margin around unpredictable road users.
- Reassess whenever the environment changes.
