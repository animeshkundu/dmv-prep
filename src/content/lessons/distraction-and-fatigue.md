Distraction includes anything that takes eyes, hands, or thought away from driving. Set navigation, secure loose items, and handle messages before moving. A state callout provides the applicable device restrictions, but safe attention requires more than minimum legal compliance.

Fatigue slows decisions and can create brief losses of awareness. Heavy eyelids, repeated yawning, missed signs, and lane drift are warnings to stop in a safe place. Noise, open windows, or stimulants do not restore dependable alertness.

## Before departure

- Silence or stow devices.
- Arrange controls and mirrors.
- Plan breaks before alertness declines.
- Let a passenger handle non-driving tasks.
