Pavement markings separate opposing flows, organize lanes moving in the same direction, identify edges, and reserve space for specific uses. Interpret color, pattern, and position as a combined message.

A broken line generally describes a boundary that may be crossed when the maneuver is permitted and safe. A solid line calls for greater restriction or caution. Painted islands and buffer areas are space to keep clear, not convenient passing lanes.

## Marking scan

- Look far enough ahead to see where a lane begins or ends.
- Recheck arrows before committing to a turn lane.
- Treat a lane-use marking as applying to the lane containing it.
- Avoid drifting across markings while searching or signaling.
