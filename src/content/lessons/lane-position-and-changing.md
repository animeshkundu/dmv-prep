Good lane position creates space from hazards while keeping the vehicle predictable. Avoid riding a lane line or making small unexplained drifts that force nearby drivers to guess your intent.

Before changing lanes, search ahead, check mirrors, signal, check the blind area, and confirm the gap remains open. Move gradually enough to maintain control but decisively enough that others can understand the maneuver.

## Space check

- Avoid moving into another driver's following gap without room.
- Recheck the new lane after signaling.
- Cancel the maneuver if the gap closes.
- Establish a centered path before ending the signal.
