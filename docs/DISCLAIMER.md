# Disclaimer

**DMV Prep is an unofficial study aid.** It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any state Department of Motor Vehicles, Department of Public Safety, Secretary of State office, or any other government agency.

## What this means

- **Study aid, not the source of truth.** The questions, explanations, and per-state facts here are meant to help you prepare. They do not replace your state's official driver handbook, the actual knowledge test, or guidance from your licensing agency.
- **Always verify against your official handbook.** Rules, fees, required documents, ages, appointment policies, and test formats change and vary by state. Before you rely on anything here, confirm it against your state's current official handbook and `.gov` site. Every state page links to the official source.
- **Original, cited content.** Every question is original and grounded in the state's public driver handbook, with the handbook section or statute cited. We do not reproduce real exam questions, and passing our practice tests does not guarantee passing the official test.
- **Freshness stamps.** Content shows a "verified as of" date. Because rules change, treat older stamps with extra care, especially when using the app offline where cached data may lag the live source.
- **No official endorsement implied.** We use agency names only to tell you which office handles licensing in your state. We do not display agency seals or logos, and nothing here should be read as an official communication.

## No warranty

This app is provided "as is," without warranty of any kind. We work hard on accuracy (original questions, per-item citations, and an adversarial cross-model verification pass), but we cannot guarantee that every fact is current or error-free. You are responsible for verifying requirements with your official state agency.
